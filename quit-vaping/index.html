01 · Urge surfing

An urge rises, peaks, and passes if you wait it out.

  1. RiseA cue, such as after a meal, in the car, or during work stress.
  2. PeakNotice the physical tension without acting on it.
  3. PassCravings usually ease within minutes when you do not act on them.

Bowen & Marlatt (2009), Psychology of Addictive Behaviors; Smokefree.gov, Understanding Withdrawal

App features

Tools for counting days, savings, and urges.

Quit Vaping counts your vape-free days and pod savings, logs daily check-ins, and includes a paced breathing timer.

Disposable & Pod Savings

Enter the price of your disposable or vape pod and how many days it typically lasts. See daily, weekly, and yearly savings calculated down to the cent.

Withdrawal Reference Timeline

Follow general reference milestones alongside your day count, reflecting research that nicotine withdrawal symptoms usually peak in week 1 and ease over 2 to 4 weeks (Hughes, 2007).

Urge-Surfing Breathing

When a craving hits, open the SOS timer for paced 4-6 or 4-7-8 breathing while you wait for the urge to rise and pass.

Visual Progress Calendar

See your vape-free days across the month with simple color coding. If you slip, log it calmly without guilt and keep going.

Vape-Free Time Counter

See the days, hours, minutes, and seconds since your last puff, updated continuously on your device.

Local-First Privacy

No email required, no social profile, and no third-party ad networks. Your check-ins and savings logs stay on your device.
