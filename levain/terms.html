6. Baking, food safety and your own judgement

This matters more in a baking app than the usual boilerplate, so it is stated plainly.

Levain is a timer and a record-keeping tool. The durations, temperatures, hydration figures and baker's percentages it shows are guidance based on what you enter, not instructions from a food safety authority. Fermentation is affected by your flour, your starter, your room and your oven, none of which the app can measure.

You are responsible for the safety of what you bake and for the safe use of your oven and kitchen. Use your own judgement about dough that has over-proofed, been left out too long, or looks or smells wrong, and follow the food safety guidance that applies where you live. Never leave a heat source unattended because an app said a timer had not finished. If you have a food allergy or a medical condition, do not rely on the app's ingredient or percentage calculations in place of professional advice.

Alarms depend on iOS, on the permissions you grant and on your device being switched on and charged. They are reliable, but they are not a safety device — do not rely on Levain alone for anything where a missed alert would be dangerous.
