Frequently Asked Questions

How do I switch between a 30-Day T-Break and Quitting for Good?

Open Unfog, go to Settings → Goal Mode, and switch anytime between a 30-Day Tolerance Break and Quit for Good without losing your check-in history.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new iPhone?

Open Unfog, navigate to Settings → Restore Purchases, and ensure you are signed into the same Apple ID used for your original purchase.

Where is my data stored?

100% of your logs live locally on your iPhone for maximum privacy. We never upload your cannabis habits or notes to external cloud servers.
