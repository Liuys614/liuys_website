Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · T-Break & Quit Weed: Unfog

T-Break & Quit Weed: Unfog ("Unfog", "we", "our") is built on a simple principle: your health, tolerance break, and cannabis recovery journey belong strictly to you.

1. 100% On-Device Data Storage

All streak timers, daily check-ins, craving logs, trigger notes, and dispensary spending calculations are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's secure on-device storage. We do not operate external cloud servers that store your personal habit logs.

2. Zero Account Requirement

Unfog never asks for your name, email address, phone number, or social login. You can use the entire app anonymously from the first launch.

3. No Third-Party Advertising or Data Brokers

We never sell, rent, or share user data with advertisers or data brokers. Unfog contains zero third-party advertising SDKs.

4. Anonymous Crash & Diagnostic Signals

To keep the app crash-free, we may collect strictly anonymous, non-identifiable technical diagnostics (such as iOS version and app stability events) that contain zero personal health or journal content.

5. Deleting Your Data

Because your data lives on your device, deleting the app or resetting your progress inside Settings permanently erases all local records immediately.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at liuys614@gmail.com.
