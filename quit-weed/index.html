Abstinence timeline

What research shows after stopping cannabis

General milestones from published research on cannabis withdrawal, sleep, and CB1 receptor recovery.

First nights

Sleep disruption

Stopping heavy use can shorten total sleep and slow-wave sleep at first (Bolla et al., 2008).

First days

Withdrawal symptoms

Irritability, anxiety, sleep trouble, and appetite changes can appear (Budney et al., 2004).

Within days

CB1 recovery begins

Brain CB1 receptors begin recovering within days of stopping (Bonnet & Preuss, 2017).

Early urges

Cravings rise and pass

Urges come and go, and tend to grow weaker over time without acting on them (Smokefree.gov).

First weeks

Withdrawal time course

Emotional and behavioral symptoms follow a typical withdrawal course (Budney et al., 2004).

Daily logs

Pattern awareness

Logging triggers in the app helps spot stress, boredom, or sleep cues.

About 4 weeks

CB1 density normalizes

CB1 receptors return to normal levels after about 4 weeks without cannabis (Hirvonen et al., 2012).

Ongoing

Sustained routine

Keep counting weed-free days and estimated money saved on your device.

Sources: Hirvonen et al. (2012), Mol Psychiatry; Budney et al. (2004), Am J Psychiatry; Bonnet & Preuss (2017), Subst Abuse Rehabil; Bolla et al. (2008), Sleep; Bowen & Marlatt (2009), Psychol Addict Behav; Smokefree.gov. General research timelines only; Unfog counts days and logs and does not measure body signals.
