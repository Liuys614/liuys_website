Standard kitchen timers count down once. Sourdough takes 24 hours across multiple delicate stages. Here is what Levain solves.

The Problem

Kitchen timers only run once. You have to reset alarms for autolyse, 4 folds, shape, and cold proof.

How Levain Solves It

One Continuous Schedule

Chain every stage from mix to bake into a single timeline. Shows exact clock times for every step, even crossing midnight.

The Problem

How Levain Solves It

Alarms That Cut Through Silent

Questions

Will the alarms wake me during an overnight proof?

Yes. Stage alerts use AlarmKit, so they ring through silent mode and Focus the way a Clock alarm does. Loud alarms are set per stage, so you can keep the overnight cold retard quiet and make the oven timer loud.

What happens when a stage runs past its timer?

The clock keeps counting up instead of resetting. Dough is ready when it is ready, so Levain waits and tells you how far past the planned time you are.

Does Levain need an account or an internet connection?

No. There is no account and no sign-up, and your recipes, bakes and photos stay on your iPhone. Details are in the 隱私權政策.

How is baker's percentage calculated?

Percentages update as you edit ingredients, with levain counted as half flour and half water — the standard assumption for a 100% hydration starter.
