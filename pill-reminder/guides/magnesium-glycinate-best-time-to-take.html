Best Time to Take Magnesium Glycinate: Morning or Night?

Published 2026-10-27 · 6 min read · Supplement & Medication Series
Quick Answer: What Time of Day to Take Magnesium Glycinate: Many people take magnesium glycinate 30 to 60 minutes before bed as part of an evening sleep routine, while others take it in the morning with breakfast since it is generally non-sedating and gentle on digestion. According to the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements, the upper limit for magnesium from supplements alone is 350 mg per day for adults unless advised otherwise by a doctor.

Magnesium glycinate (magnesium bound to the amino acid glycine) is a popular form of magnesium supplement because many people find it gentler on digestion than forms like oxide or citrate.

A common question is whether you should take magnesium glycinate in the morning or at night. There is no single required time of day: timing depends mainly on your routine and whether you are taking it as part of a bedtime habit, with morning meals, or for general daily intake.

Why Magnesium Form Matters: Glycinate vs. Citrate vs. Oxide

Different magnesium compounds behave differently in the digestive tract:

  • Magnesium Oxide: Less well absorbed than chelated or soluble forms and more likely to draw water into the intestines, which is why it is commonly used as an osmotic laxative.
  • Magnesium Citrate: Well absorbed in water, though higher doses can loosen stools for many people.
  • Magnesium Glycinate (Bisglycinate): Magnesium bound to glycine. Many people choose it because it tends to be easier on the stomach, although high doses of any magnesium supplement can still cause diarrhea and abdominal cramping (NIH ODS).
What Studies Show About Magnesium, Glycine, and Sleep: Direct research on magnesium glycinate for sleep is limited. In a small double-blind clinical trial of 46 older adults, 500 mg of magnesium daily for 8 weeks improved insomnia scores, sleep efficiency, and time to fall asleep compared with placebo (Abbasi et al., 2012). Separately, Bannai et al. (2012) reported that 3 g of pure glycine before bed reduced daytime fatigue in a small group of partially sleep-restricted volunteers, though a typical magnesium glycinate pill contains far less glycine than 3 g.

Best Time to Take Magnesium Glycinate for Anxiety vs. Before Bed

When deciding when to take magnesium glycinate, consider how it fits your daily schedule and how you feel after taking it:

  • In the Morning: Taking magnesium glycinate with breakfast works well if you already take other morning supplements and prefer not to add another step at bedtime. Most people do not find it noticeably sedating during the day.
  • Before Bed: Many people take magnesium bisglycinate 30 to 60 minutes before bed as part of a calming evening routine, especially if they are trying to support sleep or find that it helps them wind down.

What to Know Before Taking Magnesium Glycinate & How to Take It

Before taking magnesium glycinate, check the supplement facts panel for elemental magnesium rather than the total compound weight. According to the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements, the adult Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA) is roughly 310 to 420 mg per day from food and supplements combined depending on age and sex, while the upper limit for magnesium from supplements alone is 350 mg per day for adults unless a doctor prescribes more. Swallow capsules whole with water, or stir powder into 4 to 6 oz of water. Can you take magnesium glycinate during the day? Yes, most people tolerate daytime doses without drowsiness.

Can You Take Magnesium with Other Vitamins?

  1. Taking Alongside Vitamin D3: Many people take vitamin D3 with a daytime meal and magnesium in the evening. While magnesium plays a role in vitamin D metabolism in the body, you do not need to take them at the exact same time.
  2. High-Dose Minerals: Very large supplemental doses of calcium or zinc may compete with mineral absorption, so some people prefer spacing high-dose mineral supplements a couple of hours apart.
  3. Prescription Medications: Magnesium can bind to certain antibiotics (such as tetracyclines and fluoroquinolones), bisphosphonates, and thyroid medications. Ask your doctor or pharmacist about spacing, and talk to a doctor before supplementing if you have kidney disease.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

When should you take magnesium glycinate: morning or night?

Many people take magnesium glycinate 30 to 60 minutes before bed as part of an evening sleep routine. If you prefer taking supplements with breakfast or want to keep your schedule simple, taking it in the morning is also fine since it is generally non-sedating.

When should I take my magnesium glycinate with food or on an empty stomach?

You can take magnesium glycinate with or without food. Many people find it gentler on the stomach than other forms, though taking it with breakfast or a light evening snack can help prevent mild nausea if your stomach is sensitive.

When is the best time to take magnesium glycinate?

The best time is whichever time of day you can stick with consistently. Evening before bed is popular for people focusing on sleep habits, while morning with breakfast works well for a daily supplement routine.

How many hours before bed should you take magnesium glycinate?

Most people who take it for sleep take it about 30 to 60 minutes (or up to 1 to 2 hours) before going to bed, though research on exact timing is limited.

Can I take magnesium glycinate in the morning?

Yes, you can take magnesium glycinate in the morning with breakfast. For most people it does not cause daytime drowsiness, though if you notice it makes you sleepy, you can move your dose to the evening.

What is the best time to take magnesium glycinate for weight loss?

Magnesium glycinate is not a weight-loss or fat-burning supplement, and current research does not support taking it to reduce belly fat or cortisol. If you take magnesium glycinate for general nutrition or sleep support, take it with a meal or in the evening at whatever time you will remember consistently.

Can I take magnesium glycinate on an empty stomach?

Yes. Many people tolerate magnesium glycinate on an empty stomach better than magnesium oxide or citrate. However, taking it with a light meal or snack can lower the chance of mild stomach upset.

Will taking magnesium glycinate in the morning make me drowsy?

For most people, no. Magnesium glycinate is not a heavy sedative, and taking it in the morning usually does not cause grogginess. If you find it makes you feel overly relaxed, shift your dose to bedtime.

What is the recommended daily dosage of magnesium glycinate?

According to the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements, the total daily RDA for adults is about 310 to 420 mg from food and supplements combined depending on age and sex, and the upper limit for magnesium from supplements alone is 350 mg per day unless a doctor advises otherwise. Check the label for elemental magnesium and talk to a doctor before taking higher doses.

How long does it take for magnesium glycinate to work for sleep?

Responses vary, and evidence is limited. Some people feel more relaxed within a few days, while in a small 8-week trial in older adults, participants took magnesium daily for several weeks before insomnia scores were measured against placebo.

When to take magnesium glycinate for sleep?

Many people take magnesium glycinate 30 to 60 minutes before bedtime as part of an evening wind-down routine. Evidence on exact timing is limited, and adults should keep supplemental magnesium at or below 350 mg per day unless directed by a doctor.

When to take magnesium bisglycinate: morning or night?

Magnesium bisglycinate (another name for magnesium glycinate) is commonly taken at night by people hoping to support sleep, or in the morning with food for general daily intake. Most people find it gentle on digestion and non-drowsy during the day.

What to know before taking magnesium glycinate?

Check the elemental magnesium dosage on the label, keep supplemental intake at or below 350 mg per day unless supervised by a doctor, and talk to your doctor if you have kidney problems. Space your dose apart from antibiotics or thyroid medications as advised by a pharmacist, and watch for diarrhea or cramping at high doses.

What time of day should you take magnesium glycinate?

Take magnesium glycinate 30 to 60 minutes before bed if you are taking it as part of an evening sleep routine, or in the morning with breakfast if you prefer taking supplements earlier in the day.

What is the best time to drink magnesium glycinate powder?

Many people drink magnesium glycinate powder 30 to 60 minutes before bed, stirred into 4 to 6 ounces of water. Using a modest amount of water can help avoid waking up to use the bathroom at night.

Can you take magnesium glycinate during the day?

Yes, you can take magnesium glycinate during the day with breakfast or lunch. Unlike prescription sleep medications, it does not typically cause grogginess or heavy sedation for most people.
