Agricola Solo Mode Rules: 3 Food per Worker & Target Score Table

Published 2026-11-12 · 6 min read · Agricola Companion Rules Series
Quick Answer — Agricola Solo Mode Rules & Targets: In solo Agricola, each adult family member eats 3 food units per harvest (up from 2 in multiplayer), the 3-Wood space yields only 2 Wood per round, and you start with 0 food. A solo campaign runs up to 8 games starting at a 50 VP target (scaling: 50, 55, 59, 62, 64, 65, 66, 67). Beating the target lets you keep 1 permanent Occupation for free and bank 1 starting food for every 2 bonus points.

Playing Uwe Rosenberg's worker-placement classic Agricola solo is one of the tightest economic puzzles in tabletop gaming. While the 14-round board game progression remains unchanged, key rule modifications dramatically increase tension—most notably the punishing 3-food feeding requirement and the 2-wood accumulation throttle.

Whether playing a standalone practice run or embarking on the official 8-game solo campaign series, understanding these mathematical differences is vital. Paired with our complete Agricola scoring rules guide, this reference helps you clear the 50+ Victory Point barrier without taking ruinous Begging Cards.

The 8-Game Solo Campaign: Target Scores & Progression

The official Agricola rules outline an 8-game solo campaign where your farm carries over legacy bonuses between matches as target scores escalate from 50 to 67 VP:

  1. Permanent "Frozen" Occupations: Meeting or beating the target score allows you to choose one played Occupation card to carry over. That occupation enters play face-up and active at Round 1 of all subsequent campaign games for free, without spending an action. By Game 8, you start with 7 permanent occupations in play simultaneously.
  2. Food Surplus Carry-Over: For every 2 points scored above the target, you start the next game with 1 bonus food (rounded down). Scoring 56 VP in Game 1 (target 50, +6 excess) awards 3 starting food in Game 2.

Failing to reach the target score ends the campaign immediately. Games past Game 8 scale by +1 VP per match (Game 9 = 68 VP, Game 10 = 69 VP).

The 3-Food Challenge: Surviving Harvest 1 on 0 Starting Food

In multiplayer, a 2-person family needs 4 food but starts with 2 food (net deficit of 2). In solo mode, your 2 workers require 6 food with 0 starting food by Round 4. Three reliable lines solve this opening crisis:

  • The Fireplace Line: Collect 2–3 Clay in Round 2, purchase the Fireplace, and take the 3–4 sheep that appear in Stage 1 or 2. One sheep converts to 2 food, providing flexible feeding as detailed in our Agricola feeding and begging cards strategy.
  • The Grain & Clay Oven Line: Take Grain in Round 1, plow in Round 2, sow in Round 3, and build a Clay Oven (3 Clay, 1 Stone) in Round 4. Baking 1 harvested grain yields 5 food, virtually guaranteeing safety.
  • Delayed Fishing Accumulation: Because opponents cannot block spaces, Fishing accumulates 1 food every round. Sweeping Fishing in Round 4 yields 4 food in a single worker action.

Overcoming the 2-Wood Forest Limit

Because the Forest space accumulates 2 wood instead of 3, you lose 14 total wood across the game. Manage this shortage with three rules:

  • Rooms Before Fences: Reserve all early wood for building 1–2 wooden rooms (5 wood + 2 reed each) to facilitate Stage 2 family growth.
  • Single-Turn Fencing: When fencing pastures, build all boundaries in one turn using our optimal 2×3 four-pasture 15-fence layout to score full 4 pasture VPs and clear 6 empty tiles.
  • Batch Non-Wood Resources: Let Reed and Clay accumulate for 3–4 rounds before harvesting, saving action economy for wood gathering.

Blueprint for a 50+ Point Game 1

Reaching 50 VP in Game 1 without legacy occupations requires balanced scoring across all categories:

  1. Stone Rooms (10 VP): Expand to 5 rooms and renovate to Stone (2 VP per room).
  2. Family Members (15 VP): Reach the 5-member family cap (3 VP per worker).
  3. Livestock Baseline (7–10 VP): At least 1 sheep, 1 boar, and 1 cattle to avoid -1 penalties, plus 4 pastures (4 VP).
  4. Crops & Fields (7–9 VP): Plow 3–4 fields (2–3 VP) and reach 2+ VP thresholds on grain and vegetables.
  5. Major Improvements (8–12 VP): The Well (4 VP), Clay/Stone Oven (2–3 VP), and bonus cards easily seal victory over 50 VP.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much food does each family member eat in Agricola solo mode?

In solo Agricola, adult family members require 3 food units each during the Harvest phase (Rounds 4, 7, 9, 11, 13, and 14), compared to 2 food in multiplayer games. A newborn born in the current round requires 1 food for their first harvest.

What are the target scores for the 8-game Agricola solo campaign?

The target scores are: Game 1: 50 VP; Game 2: 55 VP; Game 3: 59 VP; Game 4: 62 VP; Game 5: 64 VP; Game 6: 65 VP; Game 7: 66 VP; Game 8: 67 VP. Each subsequent game beyond Game 8 increases the target by +1 VP.

How does occupation freezing work in Agricola solo play?

When you successfully reach or exceed the target score for a campaign game, you choose one Occupation card played during that game. In all subsequent campaign games, that card begins in play face-up at Round 1 for free without costing an action.

What happens if you fail to reach the target score in a solo game?

If your final score is even 1 point below the target score, you lose and the solo campaign ends immediately. Your legacy occupation bonuses and saved high scores are reset for the next fresh campaign.
