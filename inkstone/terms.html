What Inkstone does

Inkstone lets you import a PDF, place signature, initials, date, and text fields on it, and generate an encrypted, tamper-evident signing link. Your recipient opens that link in any mobile or desktop browser and signs there — they need no app and no account.

Every signing flow requires an explicit confirmation that the signer consents to sign electronically, and every completed document carries an audit page recording SHA-256 digests of the file before and after signing.

Ninety-day retention applies to every plan

Every stored document is deleted 90 days after it is signed — on the free tier and on Inkstone Pro alike. Pro does not extend storage. This is deliberate: signed documents routinely contain other people's passport numbers and national identity numbers, and we would rather not hold that indefinitely.

The app shows the remaining days on each document so you can save your own copy before it goes, and the automatic copy to Files and iCloud Drive takes care of this for you when it is left on. After deletion we cannot recover the file for you. Treat Inkstone as a way to get documents signed, not as your long-term archive.
