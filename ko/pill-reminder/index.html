Frequently asked questions

Where is my medication data stored?

Your medicines, schedules, inventory counts, and dose history are stored locally on your device. The app does not upload this information to the developer.

Do I need an account?

No. Pill Reminder works without registration, login, or a network connection.

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings and enable iCloud Backup to save a snapshot to your own iCloud account, or use Export Medication List / Export Dose History to save a CSV or PDF file.

How do I delete my data?

Remove individual medicines or reminders in the app, or use Delete All Local Data in Settings. Deleting the app removes its local app container, subject to any device backup or exported file you created.

Is this medical advice?

No. Pill Reminder is a self-directed reminder and tracking tool, not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or drug-interaction guidance. Always follow your prescriber's or pharmacist's instructions.
